We organize this paper as follows:

Section \ref{cloaking} provides a
general description of cloaking and explains how coordinate transformations can
manipulate light.

Section \ref{helmholtzexample} illustrates the main concept of
transformation optics through a simple example using the Helmholtz equation.

Section \ref{numerics} presents numerical experiments that validate the
theory.

Section \ref{maxwell} briefly explains Maxwell's equations and discusses the
challenges of applying transformation optics to these PDEs.

Section \ref{geodiff} introduces differential geometry tools that enable us to
handle coordinate transformations of Maxwell's equations.

Section \ref{cloakformula} synthesizes these concepts and derives
the formula for an invisibility cloak.
